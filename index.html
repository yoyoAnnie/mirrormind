A note from me我想对你说的

I wanted to know who I was before I had to decide what to do next. 我想在决定下一步做什么之前，先弄清楚自己是谁。

When you’re choosing between two paths, it rarely feels like a math problem. It feels like a question about who you’re going to become. I kept getting stuck there, so I built a small place to think it through, one honest line a day. 当你在两条路之间犹豫时，那很少像一道数学题，更像是在问：我将要成为谁。我总是卡在这里，所以做了一个小小的地方，让我可以慢慢想，每天只写一句真话。

See how it works看看它怎么运作

One line a day每天一句

No long forms. Over weeks, a quiet picture of you appears.不用填长表格。几周之后，一幅安静的关于你的画像会慢慢出现。

One small experiment一个小实验

A conversation, a shadow day, a two-week trial. Real life, not guessing.一次谈话、一天跟岗、两周试试看。用真实生活，而不是猜。

Diary stays private日记只属于你

You can share a card or a milestone. Your worries stay with you.你可以分享一张卡或一个里程碑，而你的心事只留给自己。

Why it’s built this way为什么这样设计

None of this is new. People have been noticing it for decades.这些并不是新发现，研究者已经留意了几十年。

Too many choices freeze us选择太多，人会僵住

In a well-known jam study (Iyengar & Lepper, 2000), 24 flavours led to about 3% buying. With 6 flavours, about 30% did. Long lists make comparison louder, so I narrow your options before asking you to commit.在著名的果酱实验里（Iyengar 和 Lepper，2000），24 种口味时大约 3% 的人购买，6 种时约 30%。选项越长，比较的声音越大，所以我会先帮你收窄，再请你做决定。

Good enough is allowed「足够好」就可以了

Herbert Simon (1955) argued that people don’t find the perfect option, they find one that’s good enough. Decision fatigue is real too (Baumeister et al., 1998). So the next step only has to be testable, not perfect on paper.Herbert Simon（1955）提出，人们并不是在找完美的选项，而是在找一个足够好的。决策疲劳也是真实存在的（Baumeister 等，1998）。所以下一步只需要可以去试，不需要在纸面上完美。

It should feel like your choice它应该是你自己的选择

Self-determination theory (Deci & Ryan, 2000) says we do better when we feel autonomous and capable. MirrorMind never hands you a verdict. It hands you a small thing to try, and you decide what to make of it.自我决定理论（Deci 和 Ryan，2000）认为，当我们感到自主、感到自己有能力时，状态会更好。镜心从不给你判决，只给你一件可以试试的小事，怎么理解它由你决定。

What this is not. It isn’t therapy, it doesn’t diagnose, and it doesn’t replace a counsellor. If things feel heavy, please talk to a campus service, a counsellor, or emergency services where you live.它不是什么。它不是心理治疗，不做诊断，也不能代替咨询师。如果你觉得很沉重，请联系学校的咨询服务、咨询师，或你所在地的紧急服务。

MirrorMind is an early concept. Statistics from my business plan should be verified against the original reports before formal use. This page collects no personal data.镜心目前还是一个早期概念。我商业计划书中的统计数据，在正式使用前需要对照原始报告核实。本页面不会收集任何个人数据。
